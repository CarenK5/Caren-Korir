Electricity theft detection

Python, pandas | Prototype

Electricity theft is one of the biggest causes of power loss at utilities. This tool flags customers whose consumption falls sharply and persistently, compared with both their own history and their peer group, so seasonal dips are not mistaken for theft.

  • Each alert has a risk score, an estimated monthly kWh loss and a plain-language reason.
  • Alerts are leads for field inspection, not accusations.
  • Tested on synthetic data: it found 19 of 20 planted cases with no false alarms.
